5 · Typography — why the light weights go

The users you are designing for may have drug-induced reading difficulty. Pentosan polysulfate carries a documented pigmentary maculopathy risk — reported prevalence rises with cumulative dose. The reported symptoms are specifically prolonged dark adaptation, night-vision difficulty and difficulty reading, often with normal visual acuity. Light-stroke serifs and weight-300 body text are the worst possible setting for these readers.
✕ Brand spec applied to product
Cormorant Garamond 300 at 15px. Elegant on a hero. In a form label at arm's length, in a waiting room, for someone whose contrast sensitivity is degrading — unreadable.
DM Sans 300 at 14px in Muted. The hex passes AA at 4.95:1, but weight 300 thins every stroke and the effective legibility is well below what the number implies.
✓ Product spec
DM Sans 400 at 18px in Charcoal. 12.96:1. This is the patient app baseline. Scales to 200% without breaking layout.
DM Sans 500 at 18px in interactive-ink for anything the user must act on. 8.18:1 — comfortably AAA.
Where Cormorant still belongs
Relief at the root.
Weight 500 or above, size 24px or above, brand moments only — login, onboarding, empty states, print report headers. Never a form label, never a table cell, never a data value.

7 · Standing rules

  • Patients never see AI narrative. Do not design a place for it. India's telemedicine guidelines prohibit AI counselling patients; the US non-device exclusion runs to health professionals only.
  • Every AI output on a doctor screen carries inline source citations, a link to the raw data, and a visible "AI-generated — not medical advice" label.
  • Meaning is never carried by colour alone. Every state gets an icon and a text label.
  • Patient app: 18px body minimum, weight 400 minimum, AAA contrast, 48px touch targets, real dark mode, 200% text scaling without layout break.
  • MR app is offline-first. Offline is a normal state with its own design, not an error screen.
  • Every component needs a press state. There is no hover on a phone. A component documented only for hover is not done.
  • Aggregates under 5 patients render as an em dash, never a number.
  • Never: Cormorant below 24px or below weight 500 · DM Sans at weight 300 · Sage #8AAB8A behind text · any untested colour pair · a screen designed only in its happy state.
