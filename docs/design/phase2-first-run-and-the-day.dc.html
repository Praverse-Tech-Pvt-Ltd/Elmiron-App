Elmiron Field Phase 2 · First run & the day

Twenty-eight screens, one thumb.

First run, the daily loop, self-service, and the four states that get skipped. Frames are 390 × 844 at 1:1 on the Phase 1 tokens. Every primary action sits in the bottom third, every screen works offline, and tracking is never the headline.

Reading the board

Four flows, then states. Offline is not one of the states — it is B2, a complete working home screen. The only critical colour in the entire flow is S3.

Flow A First run the highest-abandonment surface in the product

A plain-language rationale before every OS dialog, written in the MR's interest — not one of these screens says "manager", "compliance" or "monitor". The asks are split across two days: location and notifications at sign-in, microphone deferred to the first real visit, because asking for a mic before the MR has seen any value is how you lose them.

Decisions worth arguing about
Permissions split across two days

Location and notifications at sign-in; microphone deferred to the first real visit. Asking for a mic before the MR has seen a single benefit is the abandonment moment. Costs one extra prompt on day one.

No map anywhere

Slow on a mid-range phone, costly on data, and a breadcrumb trail is the most surveillance-coded thing we could show. The route is a timeline; navigation hands off to the MR's own Maps app.

WiFi-only audio defaults on

Audio is 62% of the data bill and the MR didn't choose to generate it. Slower ingest is the price. Say if the pipeline can't take the delay.

"Who has looked" — your call

Not in the brief. If the audit log already records manager access, surfacing it to the MR costs nothing and is the strongest anti-surveillance signal in the product. It will make some managers uncomfortable.

Your two checkpoint questions

Does home lead with MR benefit or with tracking? Benefit. The first four blocks are next visit, progress, money and sync, in that order. Tracking appears once, at the bottom, as a link opened by choice. No map, no coverage score, no manager language anywhere in the flow.

Is offline designed as normal or as an error? Normal — B2 is a complete working home screen with the same structure as B1 in the neutral offline tone, and offline is deliberately absent from the four failure states. The only critical colour in the whole flow is S3, a real upload failure after five attempts.
