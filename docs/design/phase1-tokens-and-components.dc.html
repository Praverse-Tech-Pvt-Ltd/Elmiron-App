Elmiron Field Phase 1 · Tokens & components

The system, measured.

A light product theme extending the Tier 2 tokens: warm paper, white cards, one accent, two text tones. Six semantic states the reference left undefined, a reach-zone rule, and fourteen components with their full press-first state matrix. Every ratio here is computed to the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula and reproducible from the hex pairs shown.

One thing on the record

Light is the theme you asked for and it is right for review, demo and the console. It is not automatically right for a phone at 50% brightness in Indian sun. §07 sets out what an outdoor variant would need and what it would cost.

02 — Semantic states

Six states the Tier 2 reference left open.

Each ships with a mandatory glyph or word — colour never carries meaning alone. Two of them decide whether this app is trusted.

Offline reuses the muted tone with a dashed edge, never critical — it is the expected condition for most of an MR's day. Recording is terracotta, not signal red: unmissable to the MR, but a doctor glancing across a desk reads "on", not "alarm".

The rule

On an 844pt canvas the reach zone is the bottom 281pt. A screen's single primary action originates inside it, pinned — never scrolled to. Two primary actions on one screen means the screen is wrong; split it.

Destructive and irreversible actions are the deliberate exception. They sit outside the zone so a resting thumb cannot fire them.

06 — What this theme costs

Honest about the light theme outdoors.

Where it will struggle in sun
Paper at 98% luminance is a mirror.
Contrast ratios stay true on a monitor and collapse under a specular reflection. No hex value fixes that — only a darker ground does.
Hairline #F2F0E9 disappears first.
Card separation must survive on the paper-to-card step alone, which is why no control depends on a hairline for its boundary.
Battery on OLED.
A near-white ground draws measurably more than a charcoal one on the exact mid-range devices where battery anxiety is a live adoption risk.
What holds regardless
Every pair clears AA, most clear AAA.
Lowest text pair in the system is the attention foreground on its own well at 5.85. Everything else is above 6.
Meaning never rides on colour.
Solid tick, dashed ring, pulsing dot, cross — each state is distinguishable in greyscale and at arm's length.
The reach zone is theme-independent.
Nothing about one-handed use changes if the ground inverts, so an outdoor variant is a recolour of this system, not a redesign.
The recommendation, so it is on the record

Ship light as the default. Add a charcoal outdoor variant as a user-facing toggle in Settings before the pilot, and let the week-6 midday test on a real mid-range Android decide whether it becomes the default in the field. That is roughly a week of design — one recolour pass over these fourteen components and a second contrast audit — and it is cheap insurance against a field force that cannot read the screen.

07 — Open before Phase 2
Which languages ship at pilot

DM Sans has no Devanagari. If Hindi or Marathi UI is in the pilot I need a second face chosen now — Noto Sans Devanagari is the safe answer — and re-measured, because the same hex at a different stroke weight is a different legibility problem. This blocks the consent screen entirely.

Recording colour needs a doctor's eyes

Terracotta over signal red is a judgement, not a measurement. It should go into the week-4 doctor test as its own question: does the bar read as "on" or as "alarm" to someone who did not ask to see it?

Outdoor variant — yes or no

Section 06 recommends building it. It is a second state matrix and a second audit. Say the word and it lands before the pilot; say no and I will note it as an accepted risk against the field conditions in the brief.

Console density

Manager and admin run these same tokens at compact density — 13–15px base, tighter rhythm — rather than a separate system. Phase 4 is drawn that way.
