1. Summary

The backend has no engineering work left that it can complete on its own. Every remaining item is a decision that belongs to the client, legal, the hosting owner, or management. This document lists all of them in one place, ordered by urgency, with a recommendation where we have one.

Where the AI / LMS platform stands

  • Built and tested (not yet live in the app): product catalogue, learning management (courses, lessons, assignments, progress), an approved-knowledge library with two-person approval, the AI control layer (approved prompts, usage log, on/off switches, daily limits), and the complete logic for the first AI feature — product questions answered only from approved, cited material.
  • Safety built in: the AI never answers without an approved source; questions containing patient details are refused before anything is sent to an AI provider; no conversation text is stored.
  • What stops it going live: decisions #4 and #5 below — where the AI service runs, and which AI provider may be used (including whether data may leave India).

If only five decisions can be made now

  1. #1 UCPMP sample cap — the build is set to fail on 6 November.
  2. #3 Registered legal name on the consent notice — every consent captured without it is permanently defective and cannot be corrected later.
  3. #2 Company-level settings — due 31 October; also needed for per-company AI controls.
  4. #4 and #5 AI hosting and AI provider — together these unblock the whole AI platform.
  5. #14 Scores in training and coaching — unblocks assessments and certification.

8. Glossary

UCPMP
Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices — the Indian code limiting samples and gifts to doctors.
PV (pharmacovigilance)
Monitoring and reporting of side effects (adverse events) of medicines.
DPDP
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
Adverse event (AE)
Any harmful or unintended effect in a person taking a medicine.
Approved knowledge
Product and training material that a second, named person has reviewed and approved before the AI may use it.
Four-eyes approval
The person who writes content can never be the person who approves it.
