# The field app: a rep's working day

**For medical representatives.** Words in **bold** are exactly what the app shows. Some features
(Learning, live location and the AI helpers) appear only if your company has switched them on; they are
marked *if your company uses it*.

---

## Before the first day

1. **Install the app** your company gives you, and **sign in** with the email and password your admin
   sent. Forgot the password? Ask your admin to reset it; the app has no reset button.
2. **First-run steps.** The app explains each permission before Android or the iPhone asks for it:
   - **Location.** It is read when you press check in or check out. If your company uses live location,
     it is also read every few minutes during working hours while the app is on screen, and only after
     you accept the notice. Never in the background.
   - **Notifications**, so the app can tell you when something needs you.
   - **Microphone**, for your own voice notes. Nothing is recorded unless you hold the button.
   - **Battery and autostart** (Android only). Some phone makers stop apps in the background; the app
     shows the setting to change on your phone.
3. **Language.** Me → **Language**. English today; Hindi and Marathi appear as soon as their
   translations are complete.

## Your day, on Today

**Today** shows your plan for the day: the visits your manager planned, the next one at the top, and a
line saying whether everything has been sent.

- **Start the visit to *Dr …*** opens your next visit. Once you have started, it reads **Continue the
  visit to *Dr …***.
- **See today’s route** shows all of today's stops in order.
- **Find a doctor** opens the Doctors tab.
- **Add an unplanned visit**: for a doctor who is not on today's plan. Choose the doctor, the clinic and
  a reason, then **Start this visit**. It needs no approval; your manager sees it afterwards.

## A visit

1. At the clinic, press **I am here — check in**. The app reads your position once.
2. **Ask about recording** shows the doctor a screen to answer for themselves, in large print. Hand the
   phone to the doctor. **Yes, that's fine** and **No, don't record** are both normal outcomes, and
   either one finishes this step. (Visit recording itself is switched off in this release.)
3. During or after the visit you can also:
   - **Record what you left**: samples and literature, with packs and declared value. Once your company
     has set its sample limit (UCPMP), the screen shows how much of it you have used. Until then, keep
     your own count.
   - **Record a voice note**: hold the button and speak; release to finish, then save. Your own note
     for the report, not a recording of the doctor.
   - **Flag a possible side effect**: if the doctor tells you about one, write what you were told and
     send it. It reaches your company's admins in the console. Write only what the doctor told you, and
no patient's name.
4. Leaving, press **Leaving — check out**.
5. **Write your report**: what happened, what the doctor raised, the next step you promised, and the
   products discussed. **Send the report**.

## No signal? Keep working

Everything you do is **saved on the phone first** and sent when there is signal. The line on Today says
how many things are waiting; tap it to open **Saved on this phone**, which lists each item. If something will not go, it
says why, and **Try again now** sends it again. Recordings and voice notes wait for Wi-Fi unless you
choose to send them on mobile data (Me → **Mobile data**).

## Doctors

The **Doctors** tab lists the doctors in your territory. Search by name, clinic or area, and filter with
**All**, **Not seen 30d** or **On plan**. Open a doctor to see your past visits and **Add to today**.

## End of the day

Open **How today ended** (on Today or in Me). It shows the day's visits and kilometres. Add a note for
your manager if you need to, then press **End my day**. If your manager reopens the day, the app tells
you.

## Me

- **Mileage**: this month's kilometres on your claim.
- **How today ended**: the day end screen.
- **Live location**, *if your company uses it*: the notice, and **Start sharing** / **Stop sharing**.
- **What this app records**: everything the app records about you, and what it never records.
- **Mobile data**, **Language**, **Battery & autostart**.
- **Sign out**. If something has not been sent yet, the app warns you first, because signing out of a
  shared phone should not lose your work.

## If your company uses them

- **Learning**: the courses assigned to you, with lessons and short videos. Press **I have finished
  this lesson** at the end of each one.
- **Ask the assistant**: questions about how to do something in this app.
- **Product questions**: answers taken only from your company's approved material, with the source
  shown. If it has no approved answer, it says so instead of guessing.
- **AI Doctor practice**: practise a visit with an AI doctor, by typing or by speaking. Then **End the
  practice** and **Get my feedback**. It is practice only and is never a real doctor's data.

The AI helpers have a daily limit per person. They never receive patient details.

## Help

If something looks wrong, tell your manager or your company's help desk. The **Saved on this phone**
screen (tap the sync line on Today) shows a reference code for anything that failed; give them that
code.
