## Receiving stock at the depot

Check every carton against the delivery note. A carton that is damaged, wet or shows signs of heat is
set aside and reported; it is not issued to a representative.

## Carrying samples

Samples travel in the cool bag provided. They are never left in a parked vehicle. A sample that has
been exposed to heat is returned to the depot and recorded, not handed over.
