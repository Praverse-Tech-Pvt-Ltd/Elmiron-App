## Running the visit

### Opening the visit

minutes: 3

Say who you are and why you came, in one sentence. Then ask the doctor how much time they have today,
and keep to it. This is TEST content for a local rehearsal.

### Keeping to time

minutes: 3

If the doctor gave you two minutes, make one point and stop. Offer to come back with more. A short
visit that ends on time is remembered better than a long one that overran.

## After the visit

### Recording the call

minutes: 2

Complete the call report in the app before you leave the clinic car park, while you still remember
what was said. Record what was discussed, not what you hoped would be.
