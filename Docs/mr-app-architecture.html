Findings that shaped this

Risk #1 — Apple 2.5.4Apple has repeatedly rejected background-location apps for "tracking employees, which is not appropriate on the App Store" — including one where workers were contractors and the app had other features. Build MR-first, prefer geofencing, keep a foreground-only fallback.

ConsentNo B2B exemption under DPDP. The doctor is a Data Principal. Consent must be free, specific, informed, unambiguous — and withdrawable.

Minimisation by designA microphone in a consulting room necessarily captures patient talk. A broader consent clause cannot cure it — s.6(1) caps collection at necessity regardless. Hence the redaction gate.

PV screening dutyIPC §2.2.1.5: MAHs must screen digital media under their management for adverse events. You cannot record and then not look. 15 calendar days from receipt.

Unresolved contradictionA valid case report needs an identifiable patient (§2.6). DPDP says do not retain one. PV and privacy must both sign a written position before this ships.
